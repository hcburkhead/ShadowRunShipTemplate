⌁ CHALLENGE TARGET · RELAY—
DM only. Type the 6-digit sequence above (masked) and press Enter to arm it; the board appears on the player view. RANDOM arms a fresh random sequence, which also wipes the players' progress (use it if they get stuck or the pattern gets predictable). No timer or attempt cap: LOCK OUT freezes the board until you UNLOCK it, END hides it.
SYSTEM-RELAY: pick the system this challenge targets (shown on the player board). SERA (ship AI) repairs relays: an OFFLINE relay comes back ONLINE by itself once every sector (areas, lift fans, shield panels) is nominal — so damage a sector first if you want it to stay down; otherwise a crack gets it to DAMAGED. A DAMAGED relay takes TWO cracks: SERA reinitializes the system (1/2), then re-engages it → ONLINE (2/2). A relay gates that system's player controls: OFFLINE = locked, DAMAGED = throttle + battery (terminal: SHIP TERMINAL), ONLINE = everything incl. AUX (terminal: OVERDRIVE). Knock relays down in DAMAGE CONTROL → SYSTEM-RELAY; all relays start ONLINE (nothing gated). Remote: BREAKER <6 digits> · BREAKER RANDOM · BREAKER LOCK · BREAKER UNLOCK · BREAKER OFF · BREAKER TARGET <SYS> · RELAY <SYS|ALL> <ONLINE|DAMAGED|OFFLINE>.
